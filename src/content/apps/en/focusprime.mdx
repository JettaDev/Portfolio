## The problem

Habit apps count streaks and punish the day you miss; workout apps know nothing about your goals, and your calendar knows nothing about either. Progress ends up split across three places and none of them tells the truth.

## What it does

- **Today**: the day’s activities, ready to mark as done, partial or skipped.
- **Calendar and recurrence**: activities that repeat, get postponed or edited without breaking the history.
- **Plans and goals**: each plan hangs from a goal with a date and a success criterion.
- **Workouts**: routines with exercises, sets and a live session that survives switching screens.
- **Honest progress**: only what was due counts; rest days and optional items stay apart. Levels and milestones that never take points away.

## How it’s built

**Next.js 16** with the App Router and **React 19**, accessible components from React Aria and data through **TanStack Query**. Screens talk to a Zod-typed operations contract with two implementations: the real one on **Supabase** with row-level security, and a simulated one that passes the same contract tests. It deploys to **Cloudflare Workers** with OpenNext.

## The demo

It uses the simulated implementation: click “Entrar en la demostración” and you’ll get several weeks of sample activity. Changes live in memory and are lost on reload. “Escenarios de prueba” lets you simulate going offline, a service failure, a save conflict or an expired session. The interface is in Spanish.
