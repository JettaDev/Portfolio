## The problem

In an amateur 7-a-side team everything goes through the WhatsApp group: who can play on Sunday, who’s in goal, how the last match ended. Captains end up drawing the line-up on a napkin and nobody keeps track of the season’s goals.

## What it does

- **Call-ups**: each player says whether they can make it, and captains see at a glance how many still have to answer.
- **Line-up on the pitch**: 2-3-1, 3-2-1 and 2-2-2 formations, or free placement by dragging each player. Undo, substitutes and warnings when someone is missing.
- **Results**: score, scorers, assists, saves per goalkeeper and MVP. That feeds the season’s stats and leaderboards.
- **Shareable posters**: the line-up and the result export as images with the team’s colours and crest.
- **Many teams, one app**: each team only sees its own data, with admin, captain and player roles.

## How it’s built

A **React 19** and **TypeScript** SPA built with Vite and installable as a PWA. Data lives in **Supabase**: every operation is a PostgreSQL function that checks membership, and row-level security keeps teams from seeing each other’s data. Saves are versioned, so when two captains edit at once nobody overwrites the other: the app shows the conflict and lets you choose.

## The demo

It runs entirely in your browser against a simulated server with a fictional team. You can sign in as admin, captain or player to see what each one can do, and use the demo tools to trigger a save conflict or a server failure. The interface is in Spanish.
