## The problem

Chasing payments eats hours every month. Cobro sends the invoice, reminds the client when it’s due and lets you know when it’s paid.

## What it does

- Create an invoice from the previous one in seconds.
- Automatic reminders before and after the due date.
- Quarterly summary ready for your accountant.
